# Privacy policy

Focus for YouTube does not collect, send, or sell any data.

- **What it stores:** only your settings (what to hide, your schedule, break length, and when a break ends). They're saved with Chrome's `storage.sync`, so Chrome keeps them in your browser and, if you're signed in to Chrome, syncs them to your other browsers. The extension's author never receives them.
- **What it reads:** the structure of youtube.com pages, to hide parts of them, and the address of the page, to open Shorts links in the normal player. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
- **Network:** the extension makes no network requests of its own.
- **Permissions:** `storage` for your settings, and access to `https://www.youtube.com/*` to hide parts of the page.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/narinehzargarian/focus-for-youtube/issues.
